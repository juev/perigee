Perigee is a reader for text e-books. Pages are laid out by crengine, the engine of KOReader: hyphenation by the dictionary of the book's language, justified text, hanging punctuation, footnotes at the bottom of the page, two pages side by side in landscape.

Reading
• Page turning by touch zones, swipes and volume keys
• Day, night and sepia; font, size, boldness, line spacing, alignment, paragraph indent and spacing, margins
• Table of contents, bookmarks, highlights with notes, search in the book, return from a jump
• Copying, sharing, translating and looking up the selected text in the apps you have

Library
• The books in the folders you pick, with covers, authors and series
• Shelves: reading, to read, have read, favourites; collections; search and sorting
• OPDS catalogues: browsing, search, downloads
• Works without a network and without an account

Syncing with a bookOrbit server of your own
• The place you got to, both ways; a later place read on another device is offered, not jumped to
• Highlights with notes and bookmarks, both ways
• Status, rating and time spent reading
• The server's books: browsing, search, downloads; sending your books to the server

Formats: EPUB, FB2 (also in a zip archive), FB3, MOBI (not KF8/AZW3), TXT, RTF, DOC, DOCX, ODT, HTML, CHM. PDF and DjVu are not supported.

Exact positions are shared with the bookOrbit web reader for EPUB; for other formats they are exact between devices running crengine and approximate in the web reader.

No ads, no analytics. The app talks only to the servers you add. Perigee is free software under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later, and is not affiliated with bookOrbit or KOReader.
